    How a conversation becomes a product

    1. 01

      Listen

      I interview the people who do the work, not only the people who buy it. I usually bring a rough prototype to the first conversation, so we start from something concrete.

    2. 02

      Rules

      What they know becomes rules a team can build. On one retail platform, driver stock appears online only when the driver is close enough, near the route and under the order cap.

      The regulated retailer’s delivery rules→
    3. 03

      Scope

      Every requirement gets a state: V1, V2, V3 or Delete. Deferred and removed are different decisions, and the first version is leaner than the one asked for.

      Conversational surveys with a points economy→
    4. 04

      Build and prove

      I turn the scope into a prototype people can use, so the idea is tested before the budget is spent. What follows is a clear decision: build, change or stop.

    What repeats across the work

    1. A human stays in the loop.

      AI takes the steps where it adds the most value. A person completes what the model cannot do, and approves the result.

      • A reader that proposes; a person confirms
      • A preview the clinician can overrule
      • Agents that need a person to approve their work

    2. The rules are the product.

      My strongest work sits where a wrong rule costs money, breaks a law or loses a record.

      • Stock that reconciles with a state ledger
      • Patient data that never leaves the PC
      • Points that are a cash liability
      • A role model simplified to a role switch

    3. The money is inside the spec.

      Budgets, liabilities and costs are written into the product, so the client decides with a price in hand.

      • A liquidity alert on the admin dashboard
      • An AI feature costed before it was proposed
      • A build that had to cost nothing to run
