Discovery and prototype

From your idea to a usable prototype in 10 days.

Ten working days, one fixed fee, under your brand if you are an agency. You finish with a prototype people can use and a backlog your team can estimate.

01

Who it is for

A good fit

  • Agencies and consultancies that want to offer discovery without a product manager to spare.
  • Founders and teams about to commit a development budget.
  • Teams that want to test an idea with real users before they build it.

Not included

  • Months of sprint management.
  • UI design systems.
  • Development and release to production.
02

What you receive

  • At the end

    Deliverables

    • Product rules and edge cases, written for the team that will build.
    • User flows for the core journeys.
    • A usable prototype of the core flow, on sample data, with a recorded walkthrough.
    • A release-1 backlog: user stories with acceptance criteria, ready to estimate.
    • A next-release backlog, with the reason each item waits.
    • A decision log: what was decided, when and why.

    The prototype proves the logic and the flows. It is not production software.

  • During the ten days

    The sessions

    • A kickoff to agree on the goal, the users and the constraints.
    • Interviews with your client, if you are an agency, and with the people who will use the product.
    • Working sessions with your team as the rules and flows take shape.
    • A review of the prototype before the handover.
    • A handover with everything your team needs to continue.

Ten days, step by step

  1. Day 0

    From the idea

    I receive a brief or a loosely defined idea.

  2. Day 1

    Value from day one

    I bring a simple first prototype to the first interview, so the conversation starts from something concrete.

  3. Day 2

    In-depth feedback

    I interview your client, if you are an agency, and the people who will use the product. Where the prototype misses, we learn which assumptions were wrong.

  4. Day 3

    Rules and edge cases

    What people said becomes product rules and edge cases the team can build from.

  5. Days 4–5

    Flows and backlogs

    User flows for the core journeys, a release-1 backlog with acceptance criteria, and a next-release backlog with the reason each item waits.

  6. Days 6–10

    A usable prototype

    The core flow becomes a prototype people can use, on sample data. We review it with your team, and I hand everything over.

07

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost?
A fixed fee, agreed before we start. You get a quote within one working day of the first call.
What do you need from us?
Access to your client, if you are an agency, and to the people who will use the product; time from your team for the working sessions; and one contact who can answer questions within a working day.
Which time zones?
US mornings (Eastern) or European business hours: you choose. Interviews and working sessions are scheduled within those hours.
Which languages?
Spanish and English.
How do you define an AI use case or an automation?
I start from the process, not the technology. For each step, I compare AI with a person, a simple rule or plain code, and weigh its cost against the value it adds. Where AI wins, it gets a clear task, and a person completes and approves what it cannot do.
